About

Research at the intersection of perception, space, and intelligent action.

I am a computer science researcher at the University of Technology Sydney. My work explores how multimodal models can perceive, retrieve, reason, and act across geographic and physical environments.

I am particularly interested in connecting multimodal representation learning with spatial reasoning and agentic systems, and in evaluating these systems in realistic settings rather than only on isolated benchmarks.

Research

Current directions

01

Multimodal Intelligence

Learning shared representations across audio, imagery, text, and other modalities for robust real-world understanding.

02

Spatial Intelligence

Reasoning about places, environments, routes, geographic context, and spatial relationships at local and global scales.

03

AI Agents

Building agents that combine perception, memory, tools, planning, and interaction to solve tasks in complex environments.
